In Progress · Design and Build

Benchtop Electronics Cooling Test System

Designing and assembling an instrumented thermal-fluid test bench to measure forced-convection cooling across an aluminum heat sink. A 120 mm PWM fan, interchangeable 3D-printed ducts, an electrically heated aluminum test block, and Arduino-based sensing will support controlled comparisons of duct geometry and airflow.

Planned testing will compare temperature, fan speed, and thermal resistance against ANSYS Fluent CFD and thermal predictions, using discrepancies between simulation and experiment to guide design iteration.

The project will combine gear and shaft calculations, parametric CAD, ANSYS validation, additive manufacturing, and physical torque testing to compare predicted and measured performance.
