Stage 02 · 2028 Competition Mount · CAD & Packaging

Developed the 2028 competition mount through three linked revisions

I carried the 2028 competition-car eccentric differential mount from an initial concept through packaging and joint-design revisions:

  • Initial concept: modeled the first mount using the 2024 chassis as the packaging reference
  • Clearance revision: moved the differential away from the chassis tubes and halfshaft while preserving the sprocket and chain relationship, then rebuilt the surrounding geometry
  • Joint revision: changed the bolt size, hole geometry, and nearby features to improve clamping and assembly access; the current design uses M8×1.5 class 8.8 hardware

Stage 06 · Test-Mule Tab CAD, FEA & Fatigue

Applying side-specific loads to the test-mule tabs

I modeled separate left and right tabs because the calculated reactions differ by side. The FEA uses those side-specific loads, a fixed welded edge, and load applied at each bolt hole. I then used material-specific S-N data to connect the analysis factor of safety to the desired fatigue life. The tab dimensions use available nominal stock sizes to avoid custom material cutting and reduce cost.

Left tab load1,047.9 lbfper tab · 2,095.8 lbf tab-pair load
Right tab load496.65 lbfper tab · 993.3 lbf tab-pair load
  • Materials evaluated: 7075-T6 aluminum and annealed AISI 4130 steel
  • Fatigue method: material-specific S-N curve evaluation
  • Boundary condition: welded mounting edge fixed
  • Load application: 50% of each tab-pair load applied to the inner semicylindrical bolt-hole surface
  • Chain-force direction: 60° estimate used in the FEA model

Stage 07 · Test-Mule Tab Fatigue-Life Target

Set the tab FOS target from the desired fatigue life

We selected 107 cycles as the design-life target for the AISI 4130 tabs. At that life, the annealed-steel S-N curve gives a fatigue strength of approximately 336 MPa. Comparing it with the 360 MPa yield strength gives an equivalent minimum yield-based factor of safety of 360 / 336 ≈ 1.07, so I set 1.1 as the practical FEA target.
