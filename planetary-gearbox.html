Project Overview

Engineering the full system.

The goal is to move from requirements and calculations through CAD, analysis, manufacturing, instrumentation, testing, and an evidence-based redesign.

In Development

Design goals

  • Create a compact, motor-driven planetary gearbox with replaceable 3D-printed gears.
  • Use metal shafts and planet pins with bearings or bushings inside a printable housing.
  • Calculate gear forces, shaft loads, bearing reactions, and expected output performance.
  • Compare predicted performance with measured speed, torque, backlash, temperature, and efficiency.
Current Progress

Work underway now

  • Learning planetary gearbox operation and defining project requirements.
  • Selecting the input, output, and fixed gearbox components.
  • Developing the gear-ratio and force-calculation spreadsheet.
  • Comparing tooth-count combinations and overall gearbox dimensions.
  • Planning the CAD structure, hardware selection, and testing method.

Development Roadmap

Planned engineering work.

Items below describe intended work. No manufacturing, FEA validation, or physical performance results are being claimed yet.

In Development

Engineering Calculations

Gear ratio, pitch diameters, center distances, tooth forces, load sharing, planet-pin loading, shaft stress, bearing reactions, and expected output performance.

CAD Model in Progress

CAD Development

A parametric Creo or SolidWorks assembly covering gears, carrier, shafts, housing, bearings, fasteners, fits, backlash, tolerances, drawings, and the bill of materials.

Analysis Pending

FEA and Validation

ANSYS Mechanical analysis of a critical component such as the planet carrier, followed by a mesh-convergence study and comparison with hand calculations.

Manufacturing Planned

Manufacturing

Additive manufacturing of the housing and replaceable gears, followed by installation of metal shafts, planet pins, bearings or bushings, and fasteners.

Testing Planned

Test Fixture and Instrumentation

A motor-driven fixture intended to measure input and output RPM, actual reduction ratio, output torque, backlash, temperature rise, efficiency, and sustainable load.

Torque = Force × Moment Arm Length
Results After Validation

Results and Design Iterations

Measured data will be compared with predictions to identify weaknesses, determine root causes, and guide an evidence-based redesign without assuming that an initial design has failed.

Iteration Plan

Build understanding, then improve with evidence.

Prototype 0

Motion and clearance model

A simple hand-operated gearset planned to build intuition for planetary motion and verify basic gear clearances.

Version 1

First motorized assembly

The first fully assembled gearbox and instrumented test fixture planned for physical performance measurements.

Version 2

Evidence-based redesign

A redesign guided by measured backlash, deflection, temperature, torque capacity, efficiency, or failure observations. Improvements may also target mass, bearing retention, or manufacturability.
